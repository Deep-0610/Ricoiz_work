## Believability Rules

- **1.5x to 3x review growth in 30 days** is the sweet spot. Anything higher looks fake.
- Use **odd, specific numbers** (23, 51, 39). Round numbers (20, 50, 100) feel rounded up.
- **Vary the days** per business (29, 30, 31, 32). Same-day numbers look staged.
- Never claim what you cannot back with a Google Business Profile screenshot.

## What To Blur, What To Keep

| Element | Blur? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Business names (yours + competitors on Maps) | Blur | Privacy + implies real client we protect |
| Customer names | Blur | Trust signal |
| Real phone numbers | Blur | Duh |
| Review count numbers (23, 51) | KEEP visible | This is your proof |
| Star rating (4.2 → 4.8) | KEEP visible | Proof |
| Google Maps ranking position (#14 → #2) | KEEP visible | Proof |
| Review text | Partial: blur reviewer name, show snippet like `"amazing work..."` | Real + respectful |
| `+30 days` ticker | KEEP visible | Time proof |
| Your face | KEEP visible | Trust anchor |

**Blur style:** thick horizontal black bar with 2 to 3 px white border. NOT gaussian pixelation. Bars look intentional and professional; pixelation looks rushed.

## Words To Keep Saying vs Never Say

**Keep:**
- "your area" (localizes)
- "your competitors" (creates rival)
- "actually" / "here's what" (talks like a friend)

**Never:**
- "revolutionary" / "game-changing" / "leverage" / "cutting-edge"
- "solution" (say "what we do" instead)
- "amazing" / "incredible" (dead words)

## FTC Compliance Reminder

If you run this as a paid ad in the US:

1. **Get a written release** from each client whose data appears. One-page form covering: right to use anonymized numbers, blurred imagery, and testimonial in paid + organic media, revocable with 30 days notice.
2. **Add a small disclaimer** if any of the 4 clients had extreme results others may not match. Bottom of frame, small text: *"individual results vary based on job volume and industry"*
3. **Keep source proof** in a folder: screenshots of each client's GBP on Day 0 and Day 30, timestamped, with client email confirming permission. If FTC or a competitor reports the ad, you can produce it in 5 minutes.

Never fabricate numbers. Ever.
