# 01. Product Overview

## The Offer

- **Price**: $297/month + $497 setup (grandfathered for life)
- **Contract**: None — month-to-month
- **Founding 5**: Setup fee waived in exchange for video testimonial + case-study rights + before/after GBP screenshots. **Contingent on testimonial delivered within 60 days of first review — otherwise setup fee back-billed.**
- **Price ladder**: Once 20 case studies exist, new clients pay $397/month. Existing clients grandfathered at $297.

## The Guarantee

**"10 new Google reviews in your first 30 days or your next month is free."**

Reactivation campaign to a 300-500 person past-customer list reliably produces 15-30 reviews in the first two weeks. Worst-case cost of a miss: one $297 credit. Kills the primary sales objection and feeds the testimonial pipeline.

## What The Client Gets

1. **Dead-simple job intake** — owner texts customer name + phone to dedicated line
2. **3-message SMS drip** after every job:
   - Msg 1 (2hr post-job): Check-in
   - Msg 2 (2hr after Msg 1): Google review link — **only if no negative reply**
   - Msg 3 (3 days later, if link not clicked): Nudge
3. **Service recovery** — negative reply to Msg 1 → route to owner phone, suppress Msg 2 & 3
4. **Reactivation campaign** — SMS to past customer CSV, trickled at ≤50/day
5. **AI review responses** — 4-5 star auto-post, 1-3 star Slack approval (60-min SLA)
6. **Weekly competitor report** — you vs 3 named local rivals
7. **Intake activity monitoring** — 7-day inactivity SMS check-in (churn early warning)

## Target Customer (Phase 1)

**Roofing companies only — first 10 clients.**

Why roofers:
- Average job value $5K-$15K → $297/mo = laughable ROI
- Tight referral networks (supply houses, trade FB groups)
- Identical delivery templates across clients
- Least tech-sophisticated → easy sell
- Existing Close CRM outreach patterns work

**Expansion (client #11+)**: HVAC → plumbers → dentists → med spas → chiropractors → auto shops.

## Value Prop (Adam's exact framing)

**It's not a review problem, it's a remembering problem.**

- Business owner forgets to ask
- Staff forgets to ask
- Customer means to but forgets
- Result: business does great work, Google sees none of it

Our system asks for them, 3 times, on autopilot — and catches the unhappy ones before they post.

## The Math (client side)

Roofer example, avg job $5,000:
- 1 extra customer per month = $5,000 revenue
- Cost: $297/mo
- **ROI: 16.8×**

## The Retention Math

Google weights review **recency**, not just count. A business that stops generating reviews slides in local rank within 60-90 days.

Weekly competitor report makes this visible: client sees standing against 3 named local rivals every week. **Cancelling means watching competitors pull ahead.**

Reframes the product from "review count project" (done state) to "local rank maintenance" (ongoing need).
